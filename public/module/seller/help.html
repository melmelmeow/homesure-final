How to Post & List a Property
Step-by-step guide to publishing properties with verified details, photos, and Google Maps pin in Sta. Maria, Bulacan.
  1. Navigate to Post a Property in your sidebar.
  2. Fill in the property type (House, Apartment, Commercial, Lot) and set the listing price (Sale or Rent).
  3. Select the exact Barangay in Sta. Maria, enter the full street address, and pin the location on Google Maps.
  4. Upload clear, well-lit photos (front exterior, interior rooms, amenities).
  5. Submit required property documents (Deed of Sale / TCT title and Tax Declaration) for admin verification.
  6. Once reviewed (typically 1–2 business days), your listing will go live to all verified buyers!
Landowner & Title Verification
Learn about required ownership proof, LRA title verification, and how to maintain verified seller status.
  1. Go to Verification in your seller dashboard.
  2. Upload your government-issued ID (Passport, UMID, Driver's License, or National ID).
  3. Take a live selfie verification to ensure account ownership.
  4. Upload the Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT) or Condominium Certificate of Title (CCT) under your name.
  5. Our compliance team cross-checks records with the Sta. Maria Registry of Deeds and LRA database.
  6. Your verified seller badge will appear across all your listings for 12 months.
Managing Viewing Requests & Inquiries
How to communicate with interested buyers, confirm site appointments, and negotiate safely on HomeSure.
  1. When a buyer requests a viewing, you will receive an in-app notification and email alert.
  2. Open Viewing Requests to view buyer contact details, preferred date, and time slot.
  3. Click Approve or propose an alternate schedule.
  4. Use the built-in Messages channel for all pre-viewing communications.
  5. Always conduct in-person property viewings during daytime for mutual safety.
Rent Collection & Escrow Payouts
Understand automated rent payments via Xendit, security deposit holding, and landlord disbursement schedules.
  1. Tenants pay their monthly rent and security deposits through HomeSure's Xendit integration (GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer).
  2. Security deposits are held in insured escrow under contractual lease conditions.
  3. Confirmed rent payments are credited directly to your registered bank account or GCash.
  4. Track all payment history, invoices, and deduction logs in the Payments tab.
Frequently Asked Questions
How long does property listing approval take?
Property listings are reviewed by our verification team within 24 to 48 business hours. We verify that land titles (TCT/CCT) match the owner's verified name before publishing.
Can I pin my property location on Google Maps?
Yes! When creating or editing a listing in the Post a Property section, use the Google Maps locator to pin your exact street address or neighborhood in Sta. Maria, Bulacan.
How do I collect rent from tenants?
Tenants receive automated rent reminders and pay through HomeSure's Xendit integration. Funds are disbursed automatically to your registered bank account or e-wallet.
What happens if a tenant requests a refund or security deposit deduction?
You can submit documented damage claims with photo evidence through the Payments overview. HomeSure handles escrow disputes transparently according to standard tenancy agreements.
